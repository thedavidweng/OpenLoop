the signing statement in the PR as described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
Contributors retain copyright and grant David Weng rights to distribute and
sublicense covered contributions under open-source, commercial, and proprietary
terms, including closed-source paid or mobile editions.

Previously merged contributions are not automatically covered by a new CLA.
An existing Apache-2.0 contribution remains usable under that original grant,
including in a proprietary derivative, subject to its notice and license
requirements. Any earlier AGPL-only contribution without sufficient additional
rights would need separate permission before proprietary reuse.

David Weng may offer separate terms only for material for which he holds
sufficient rights. The CLA does not override third-party code, dependencies,
or asset licenses. Existing recipients retain the rights granted by their
AGPL release.

## Distribution and network use

AGPL permits commercial use. Distribution of covered binaries requires
corresponding source under the applicable provisions of section 6. A modified
version that supports remote user interaction must offer its corresponding
source to those users as described in section 13. Private modifications are
not automatically required to be published, and there is no requirement to
submit changes upstream.

For each release, provide the source matching that binary and the required
build and installation material. Include applicable third-party licenses and
notices. Review App Store distribution terms separately before publishing an
edition there.
