# Responsible Use Guidelines

OpenLoop generates music using AI models running locally on your Mac. This document provides guidance on using generated output responsibly.

## AI-Generated Content Disclosure

Many platforms and jurisdictions require disclosure when content is AI-generated. When publishing music created with OpenLoop:

- Check the platform's terms of service for AI content policies
- Disclose that the output was AI-generated when required by law or platform rules
- Keep records of your generation settings for reproducibility

## Copyright and Ownership

- OpenLoop does not guarantee that generated output is free from copyright claims.
- Generated music may inadvertently resemble existing copyrighted works.
- Users are responsible for verifying that output does not infringe on third-party rights before publishing or monetizing.
- Model weights and the ACE-Step backend are subject to their own upstream licenses (Apache 2.0 for ACE-Step).

## What to Avoid

- Do not enter lyrics, melodies, or prompts that directly copy or closely imitate copyrighted works.
- Do not use OpenLoop to generate music that impersonates a specific artist's voice or style for deceptive purposes.
- Do not use generated output in contexts that violate applicable laws on AI-generated content.

## Prompts and Lyrics

- Prompts and lyrics stay on your Mac (see [Privacy Policy](docs/privacy.md)).
- Avoid entering personally identifiable information, confidential material, or content you do not have the right to use as input.

## Commercial Use

- Generated output may be used commercially, subject to the ACE-Step model license (Apache 2.0) and applicable laws.
- Verify compliance with your jurisdiction's regulations on AI-generated content before commercial distribution.
- OpenLoop does not provide legal clearance or licensing advice.

## Reporting Concerns

If you believe OpenLoop is being used in a way that violates these guidelines or applicable laws, please open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/thedavidweng/OpenLoop/issues).
